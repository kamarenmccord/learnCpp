When naming a variable in Cpp
best practices include
-should begin with lowercase lettering
-if using only one word the enire word should be lowercase
-if the var is multi word, use of snake_case is recommended
-camelCase is also accepted
-avoid starting a new var with an underscrore _likeThis
-variables should be descriptive ex:
topSpeed, max_velocity, color_black, total_sum
